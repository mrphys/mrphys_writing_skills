## 1912.10503v1: Rapid Whole-Heart CMR with Single Volume Super-resolution

**Why:** Opens with the success statement ('successfully acquired in all 40 patients'), then numbers with mean±SD, range and p, then the derived speed-up.

**Do differently now:** '488±138 s' → '488 ± 138 s' [decided]; 'range: 200 to 889 s' → 'range: 200–889 s' [decided]; 'x2.9' → '×2.9' (or '~2.9×').

> High-resolution and low-resolution WH-bSSFP data were successfully acquired in all 40 patients. Total acquisition time for HR-WH data (488±138 s, range: 200 to 889 s) was significantly (p<0.05) higher than the LR-WH data (173±54 s, range: 66 to 302 s). The average speed-up in acquisition time was x2.9±0.8 (range: 1.5 to 5.4).
